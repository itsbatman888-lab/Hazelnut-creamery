Our Heritage

Where Desserts Are Little Moments of Happiness

Hazelnut Creamery was born in March 2026 with a simple dream: to create delicious, indulgent treats that bring people together. We started our journey with freshly made waffles, premium handcrafted ice creams, creamy ice cream shakes, delicious brownies, and irresistible sundaes.

We are Hotel Management students with a couple of years of experience in the food industry, especially in food preparation, recipes, and creating new flavours. Our background has helped us understand the importance of quality, consistency, hygiene, and making every bite memorable.

"From a warm, crispy waffle to a rich scoop of premium ice cream or a decadent Death by Chocolate brownie, every creation is prepared with care."
